🩸 Before You Register as a Blood Donor

  • check_circle You must be 18 – 65 years old.
  • check_circle Your weight should be at least 50 kg.
  • check_circle There must be a minimum 3-month gap since your last donation.
  • check_circle You should not have any fever, infection, or major illness recently.
  • check_circle Your hemoglobin should be within a healthy range.
  • check_circle You must not be under alcohol influence in the last 24 hours.
  • check_circle If you had COVID/Vaccination, take proper recovery time.
  • check_circle Double check your registration, because a small mistake can affect someone's life.

⚠ Please register only if you meet the above conditions.
